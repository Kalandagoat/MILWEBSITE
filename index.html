Health Is Wealth: How Simple Habits Can Improve Our Everyday Lives

Health is one of the most important parts of our lives. There is a saying that our health is our wealth, no amount of money or material success can replace good health or buy it back once it is lost.

It is not only about being disease-free but also about taking care of our physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

As students, we often become busy with school activities, assignments, examinations, and other responsibilities. Because of this, we sometimes forget to take care of ourselves.

We skip meals, sleep late, spend too much time on our phones, or ignore stress. And our everyday habits can affect our health in the present and in the future.

Eating nutritious food is One of the simplest ways to maintain good health. Our body needs different nutrients to grow, have energy, and function properly.

The World Health Organization (WHO, 2026) recommends eating a variety of foods, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and sources of protein.

It is also important to limit foods that contain too much sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats.

Eating healthy does not mean that we can never enjoy our favorite foods. Instead, it means learning how to balance what we eat and making healthier choices most of the time.

According to the World Health Organization (WHO, 2024), physical activity is another important part of a healthy lifestyle.

Exercise can help strengthen our muscles and bones, improve our fitness, and support our mental health.

Children and adolescents should get an average of at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity every day.

Physical activity does not always have to be a serious workout. Walking, dancing, playing sports, riding a bicycle, or even doing household chores can help us stay active.

Students experience different kinds of pressure, such as academic responsibilities, family problems, friendships, and personal struggles.

Sometimes, we may feel overwhelmed or emotionally tired. Taking care of our mental health is equally important.

According to the World Health Organization (WHO, 2024), anxiety and depression are among the common mental health concerns among adolescents.

This is why it is important to recognize our feelings and seek support when needed.

Talking to a trusted friend, family member, teacher, or professional can help us cope with difficult situations.

Getting enough rest is also essential. Sleep allows our bodies and minds to recover after a long day.

Unfortunately, many students sacrifice sleep to finish schoolwork or spend more time using their phones.

Although staying up late may seem harmless, constantly getting insufficient sleep can affect our energy, concentration, and overall well-being.

Creating a regular sleeping schedule and having 8 hours sleep can help us become more productive and feel better throughout the day.

Good health begins with small and consistent choices. We do not need to completely change our lifestyle overnight.

Eating nutritious food, staying physically active, getting enough sleep, managing stress, and asking for help are simple steps that can make a difference.

We should remember that taking care of our health is just as important as achieving good grades. Our health supports everything we want to accomplish in life.

By caring for ourselves today, we are also preparing ourselves for a healthier future.

ENVIRONMENT / WRITTEN WORK

How Simple Habits Can Protect Our Environment

The environment is basically the foundation of our lives, the air we breathe, the water we drink, the food we eat, and the places where we live.

However, with rapid population growth, industrialization, and modern lifestyles, environmental problems such as pollution, deforestation, climate change, and waste have become more serious.

According to the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP, 2021), human activities are driving major changes in the environment.

Although environmental problems can feel overwhelming, individuals can still make a difference through simple daily habits.

One of the easiest ways to help the environment is to reduce waste. Many everyday products are used only once before being thrown away.

Plastic bags, bottles, food containers, and packaging contribute to the growing amount of waste in landfills and oceans.

By using reusable bags, bottles, and containers, we can reduce the amount of trash we produce.

Proper waste segregation and recycling can also help. Separating biodegradable, recyclable, and non-recyclable waste makes it easier to manage and reuse materials.

Saving energy is another simple way to help the environment. Electricity is often produced using fossil fuels, which release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

Turning off lights, fans, appliances, and electronic devices when they are not being used can reduce unnecessary energy consumption.

Using natural light during the day and choosing energy-efficient appliances can also help.

Conserving water is equally important. Water may seem unlimited, but clean and safe water is a valuable resource.

Simple habits such as turning off the faucet while brushing our teeth, fixing leaks, and taking shorter showers can prevent unnecessary water waste.

Taking care of the environment is not only about protecting nature. It is also about protecting our own well-being.

Pollution can affect the air we breathe, the water we drink, and the food we eat. Environmental destruction can also affect communities and future generations.

Another important part of environmental awareness is understanding how our actions affect other people.

Environmental problems are not experienced equally. Some communities are more vulnerable to pollution, natural disasters, and the effects of climate change.

This is why environmental responsibility should not only be an individual concern. Communities, schools, businesses, and governments must work together.

Even simple activities such as tree planting, community clean-ups, proper waste disposal, and environmental education can encourage people to become more responsible.

Protecting the environment does not require us to be perfect. What matters is that we become more aware of our habits and try to make better choices.

Small actions may seem insignificant when done by one person, but when many people participate, the impact can become much greater.

The environment belongs to everyone, and protecting it is a responsibility that we all share.

United Nations Environment Programme. Environmental awareness and action.
